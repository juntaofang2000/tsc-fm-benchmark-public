Evaluation principles

How aggregate reporting works

Equal weighting

Every dataset contributes equally to aggregate accuracy, regardless of sample count or sequence length.

Reviewed metadata

Modality, domain, size, class count, sequence length, channels, and imbalance are reviewed for all 198 datasets.

Modality views

Overall, Univariate, and Multivariate rankings are computed as distinct, auditable views.

Primary metric

Accuracy is the primary metric; Average Rank and Coverage provide complementary context.
