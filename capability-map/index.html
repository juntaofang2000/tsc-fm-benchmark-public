Reading guide

Methodology and privacy boundaries

Unit and weighting

Dataset is the analysis unit. Each contributing dataset receives equal weight within its selected condition bin.

Missing results

Missing method–dataset results are excluded and are never replaced with zero.

Paradigm taxonomy

Decision-interface groups are mutually exclusive; model-origin lenses may overlap and are descriptive only.

Public data boundary

Only aggregate condition-level statistics are published. Dataset identities and source mappings are excluded.
