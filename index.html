Evaluation Tracks

Results remain separated by adaptation setting so each score has a clear scientific meaning.

01Standard

Full official training splits with dataset-level equal weighting.

02Low-shot

Matched label budgets across methods and feasible datasets.

03Training-Free / ICL

Predictions without target-specific parameter updates.

04Frozen-Probe

Frozen representations with a standardized lightweight probe.
